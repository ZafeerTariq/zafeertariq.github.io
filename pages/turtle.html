Turtle Fight

A tower defence strategy game where player spawns turtles to attack the opponent's castle while defending their own.

Try the game here (WIP)

Platform, Technologies, Monetization

  • Developed using Cocos Creator engine for HTML5 platform.
  • Has In-App Purchases on the Yandex Game platform for in-game currency and bundles.
  • Integrated rewarded video and interstitial advertisements at meaningful points in game.

Key Features

  • Gameplay
    • Four turtle classes with unique stats like Damage, Strenght, Speed and Spawn cost.
    • Opposing turtles push each in a lane depending on their strenght.
    • Turtles advance towards the opponent's castle and deal damage.
    • Turtles can be upgraded upto level 5, increasing their stats.
    • Special emphasis on enhancing game-feel and "juice" elements.
  • Cosmetic Customizations
    • 5 unique turtle skins.
    • 2 map skins.
  • Two power-ups are available for player to use.
    • Turbo Boost: increases movement speed of all player turtles.
    • Energy Drink: increases the damage turtles deal.
  • Adaptive AI.
    • AI that takes into account the state of each lane to make spawn decisions.
    • Accounts for player level and turtle upgrades to set AI difficulty level.
